## 5. Fresh runs (RUN) — and what they establish

1. **R02's baseline witness reproduces.** `git archive origin/orx/repro-consolidation-judge-promoted-to-shared-cre | tar -x -C /tmp/anc46` then `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests` →
   `Ran 46 tests in 0.014s` / `OK`. That is R02's "source suite 46 passed at copy
   time" (`REPRO-PACK.md` L6) and CREW-EVIDENCE E3's "Ran 46 … OK", **verified by
   execution** from a ref.
2. **The retrievable ancestor is a lineage, not one revision.** The same command
   on `origin/orx/r9-consolidation-ledger-backed-laws-written-into` prints
   `Ran 35 tests … OK`. R02 inherited the 46-test revision; the r9 ref is a
   smaller, earlier one.
3. **R02's champion arm reproduces.** In the r9 tree,
   `python3 -m crew.eval --tasks tasks/real-missions.json --formation solo --hold-on-vague --shuffle-seed 7` prints
   `SUMMARY n=20 passed=20 rate=1.000 grave_errors=0 total_cost=68 resolved=4 routing_match=19/20 wall_s=0.00 formation=solo`
   — identical to R02's published champion line on `n / passed / rate /
   grave_errors / total_cost / resolved`, and different on `routing_match`
   (19/20 vs 11/20): the one field R02's own `DECISIONS-LOG.md` flags and R03's
   ADR-10 redefines. (The ancestor's SUMMARY predates the `escalations=` /
   `guard_flags=` fields R02 added.)
4. **R02 BOARD E3's defect: structurally confirmed, its crash not reachable.**
   On the r9 revision, `--formation routed` is refused by `argparse`
   (`invalid choice: 'routed'`), so the KeyError crash E3 describes cannot fire
   there — but E3's *structural* claim is visible in that same file:
   `crew/eval.py` L45–46 defines `used` **with** `"routed": "ROUTED"` and
   L57–58 redefines `used` **without** it, overwriting the router proposal
   exactly as recorded. The retrievable ref therefore predates the 2026-09-21
   sandbox whose `eval.py` lines 46–52 E3 cites.
