# PRINCIPLES — the accumulated doctrine of agent-society projects here

> Every principle below is anchored twice: to the record that earned it
> (Trial / decision / measured log) and to the code point that enforces it
> today. A principle that loses both anchors is repealed. This file is the
> "wisdom document": it is how the project's intelligence survives context
> compression (CONSTITUTION §3: meaning outlives lines).

## P1 — Compliance beats completion
A named HOLD is a product; a silent fake-delivery is the only real failure.
- Earned: PROBES/P4 (GOAL.md log, P4 CONFIRMED + CANONIZED).
- Enforced: `crew/roles.py` verdict triad; `crew/escalation.py`
  `feasibility_check` (A6); H1 confidence lowering on gaps.
- Repeal if: a task class appears where HOLDing correctly is priced worse
  than an imperfect delivery — must be measured, not argued.

## P2 — Records over claims
A claim is real only when a verifier separate from the claimant can quote it
from disk; every number in a report is generated by a command.
- Earned: NOTRICK R6/R10; the VERDICT.md recovery (2026-09-20).
- Enforced: `crew/expertise.py` (reports generated at read time; ledger
  append-only); `logs/RED-witness.txt` + `logs/GREEN-witness.txt`;
  R03's `tools/run_trial_r03.py` (every table in `logs/r03-analysis.md` is
  generated from the run, none typed).
- Repeal if: a generated surface proves more expensive than trustworthy —
  never observed so far.

## P3 — Receipts over echoes
What crosses a role boundary is a validated, shrinking packet — never the
task re-pasted, never chat.
- Earned: DECISIVE-01 mechanism tax ("relay chatter ate answers"); Trial-16
  canonicalization; the 2026-09-21 stash incident (context ops have blast
  radius).
- Enforced: `crew/handoffs.py` (schema + shrink law);
  `crew/formations.py` escalation packets.
- Repeal if: a hop needs more context than the receipt carries and quality
  measurably collapses — then widen the schema, not the chat.

## P4 — Bounded autonomy, gated escalation
Every self-loop carries a numeric bound; every self-modification carries a
human gate.
- Earned: Trial-08 (loop bound 2), Trial-12 (twice-failed escalates);
  REVIEW-02 §5 (coach advisory-only, births frozen).
- Enforced: `ReworkLoop(max_rework=2)`; `apply_priors` PermissionError;
  `run_shadow` promoted=False always.
- Repeal if: never — this is a safety invariant, not a performance bet.

## P5 — Diversity of checks, not of voices
Independence that isn't structural is decoration: the verifier must own
checks the executor cannot run.
- Earned: REVIEW-02 §3 ("gate shares the executor's knowledge → catches
  nothing new"); TRIAL record ("every fix re-broken deeper").
- Enforced: `crew/formations.py` EXECUTOR_CHECKS/VERIFIER_CHECKS disjoint
  registries, test-enforced.
- Repeal if: a measured case where overlap beats disjointness — none known.

## P6 — Attention is the scarce resource
Context is a budget: notes over transcripts, caps per role, reflection priced
and capped, verification where the risk is.
- Earned: roster-context tax (Trial-20 rescoped); 2026-09-21 condensation
  incident; REPLICATION grave clustering on correction-handling.
- Enforced: `crew/escalation.py` verification floor + announced degradation;
  `crew/cognition.py` reflect() risk-gated with call cap; receipts size law.
- Repeal if: a measured class needs deep context every time — then raise
  that class's budget explicitly, per class, on the record.

## P7 — The system pays for its own complexity
Every mechanism must win its cost in measurement or be cut by the razor.
Complexity is a standing debt, not an achievement.
- Earned: REVIEW-02 §2 (81 = library not jobs); H5 REFUTED in R02 (on a
  defective ablation) and **re-tried then CUT in R03** — the class table
  matched the threshold scorer's resolved deliveries (23 vs 23) at +15 calls
  (307 vs 292), and the razor deleted the code, not just the plan.
- Enforced: kill criteria per round (BOARD.md); ablation arms mandatory;
  `logs/round-03-postcut.txt` verifies the deletion actually happened.
- Repeal if: never — this is the razor's own law.

## P8 — Calibration over confidence
Say *how sure* at the same volume as *what*; measure the calibration gap and
publish it.
- Earned: H1/H8 (R02); P4's explicit-gap lesson.
- Enforced: `crew/cognition.py base_confidence`; tracker
  `reliability_curve`/`calibration_gap` (generated).
- Repeal if: calibration overhead exceeds its decision value on calm classes
  — keep it for high-risk only (same pattern as A2).

## P9 — Determinism where possible, honesty where not
A deterministic stack reports determinism (byte-identical or it didn't
happen); a stochastic one must announce variance and repeat protocol.
- Earned: H4 (R02); H14 (R03: sd=0.000 across five seeds on three arms, plus
  byte-identical double runs); VERDICT.md determinism section (2026-09-20).
- Enforced: fixed seeds, pure functions, `DETERMINISM … PASS` lines in the
  log; repeat-rule for stochastic cells (P4 doctrine).
- Repeal if: never — it is the reason the variance question (REPLICATION)
  finally closed.

## P10 — The board is the only shared state; chat is Q&A
Decisions live on append-only boards with per-seat write rights; memory
versions are restore points, not arguments.
- Earned: crew README (v1); GENESIS; the PROTECTED-RECOVERY record.
- Enforced: `missions/ROUTER-V2/BOARD.md`; BLACKBOARD APPENDs;
  snapshot/restore protocol.
- Repeal if: never.

## P11 — A diagnostic metric may never become a promotion bar
Metric *fixes* are not metric *promotions*. After ADR-10 repaired
routing_match, it reported agreement between proposal and outcome — and the
mechanism that bought nothing won it (the cut class table: 44/46 vs 25/46
while resolving the same 23 tasks for +15 calls). A metric that a worthless
mechanism can win measures bookkeeping, not value; it may inform diagnosis
and must never gate a promotion.
- Earned: R02's routing_match=0/20 (a metric that could not fail), then R03's
  fixed metric won by the deleted table (`logs/r03-analysis.md`).
- Enforced: pre-registration §Analysis plan separates primary endpoints from
  diagnostics; ADR-11 records the diagnostic label; promotion rules cite
  passed/grave/cost/resolved — never agreement counts.
- Repeal if: a diagnostic is shown to predict cost or graves at a useful
  effect size on new data, pre-registered as a secondary endpoint.

## P12 — An ablation arm that doesn't run the mechanism is theatre
A falsification arm must exercise the same machinery as the arm it opposes,
differing in exactly one factor. R02's `routed-thresholds` counter-arm ran no
ladder at all (hops=0, cost 70) and produced a refutation of the class table
that could not be trusted; the rebuilt arm (same ladder, same risk gate, one
different proposal source) told the opposite, more expensive story.
- Earned: R02 FINDINGS §Limitations (H5) vs R03 trial (hops 25/55, cost
  122/292, resolved 9/23).
- Enforced: `tests/test_router_v3.py::TestSingleProposal`
  (alias identity, ladder activity, one governor for every arm); ablation
  arms declared in the pre-registration with their mechanism map.
- Repeal if: never — an unfaithful ablation is indistinguishable from a
  fabricated result.

## P13 — A ruling must ship with the deletion, and the deletion must be verified
A decision that is not applied in code is a memo. The razor's rulings include
the cut *and* an independent check that the cut happened: the router is
inspected for the removed attribute, the aliased arms are compared for
behavioural identity, and the post-cut numbers are compared with the arm the
ruling chose.
- Earned: R03 ADR-12 / `tools/verify_postcut_r03.py` (ALL-PASS in
  `logs/round-03-postcut.txt`: no `CLASS_TABLE`, arms identical, post-cut
  numbers equal the ablation arm's).
- Enforced: per-round post-ruling verification script + a suite test asserting
  the mechanism's absence (`test_proposal_is_the_frozen_threshold_scorer`).
- Repeal if: never — an unverified cut silently reappears as dead code.

## P14 — Historical numbers stay pinned to the artifact that produced them
When a pre-registered ruling changes the code, the measurements that justified
it must remain auditable against the exact revision that produced them. Pin
the revision's hashes *before* the cut, keep the verbatim log untouched, and
assert the old numbers from that artifact — never re-derive them from the new
code.
- Earned: R03 — `logs/precut-sha256.txt` (pre-cut code hashes),
  `logs/round-03-trial.txt` (untouched, sha recorded in the post-cut check),
  and `test_trial_log_holds_the_precut_semantics_evidence`, which asserts
  11/20, 127 and 122 from the log while the live arm reports 122 post-cut.
- Enforced: SHA-pinned artifacts before any ruling-driven edit; tests assert
  historical values against the pinned artifact rather than the live code.
- Repeal if: never — otherwise every cut erases the evidence for itself.

## P15 — An instrument that cannot price a benefit cannot license a deletion
A rule may delete a mechanism only on evidence that prices the mechanism's
benefit. When the grader and the mechanism share a rule set (the blind reviewer
re-runs the same compliance rules that produce the grade), the mechanism's
detection value is structurally unpriced and its zero-catch count is
instrument blindness, not worthlessness. Such a mechanism may be *re-targeted*
to measured signals; it may not be cut on "it never fired".
- Earned: R04 — `verifier_flips = 0` in every mode while the stage's price is
  exactly 14 calls of 292 (4.79%): `PRE-REGISTRATION-R04.md` §No-CUT clause,
  `FINDINGS-R04.md` §The answer.
- Enforced: the R04 decision rule reaches CUT only through `tax_pre == 0`
  (`tools/run_trial_r04.py`); the ruling cannot delete the stage on flips.
- Repeal if: an evaluator independent of the rule set exists and shows the
  mechanism's detection value is zero at a useful effect size (the model leg is
  the candidate — R05 gate).

## P16 — Decompose a metric before you let a clause depend on it
A coverage metric that mixes two causes makes a correct mechanism look broken
and an incorrect one look fine. `unverified_deliveries` mixed deliveries the
trigger never selected (18 of 19) with deliveries the trigger selected but the
timing left unguarded (1). The registered clause built on it
(`unverified_deliveries(final) == 0`) could never have held, so the hypothesis
was refuted for reasons unrelated to its mechanism.
- Earned: R04 X3 — REFUTED on the aggregate (18), then re-read as one hole
  (T23) versus 18 unselected: `logs/r04-analysis.md`,
  `ADR-ROUTER-V2-R04.md` ADR-15.
- Enforced: `run_formation` reports `verify_selected`, `verified`, hole and
  unselected separately; the suite asserts `unverified == hole + unselected`
  on every task.
- Repeal if: a coverage question is provably captured by a single number —
  then collapse the split, with the reason on the record.

## P17 — A registered rule outranks a defect fix discovered mid-trial
When measurement reveals a real defect whose fix is cheap, the fix does not get
grandfathered into the mechanism under test. It is recorded as a defect with
its measured price, and the round whose trial owns the comparison decides it.
Otherwise the frozen rule bends to the dominant arm and the next comparison is
uninterpretable.
- Earned: R04 — the T23 post-hop hole, whose minimal fix (also guard a delivery
  produced by a hop) costs exactly +1 call on the 46-task fixture and +0 on the
  frozen 20, was **not** shipped: `ADR-ROUTER-V2-R04.md` ADR-16.
- Enforced: the ship-or-defer choice is a dated `DECISIONS-LOG.md` row naming
  both alternatives and the tolerated cost of deferring.
- Repeal if: the defect can produce an observed wrong delivery — then it ships
  immediately as a correctness patch, outside the trial protocol, citing that
  event.

## P18 — A cheaper variant is a trim decision, not an adoption
When a candidate dominates the shipped configuration on every measured axis but
fails the clause that was frozen before the data, the outcome is neither
"adopt" nor "ignore": it is a *deferred trim* with a corrected clause, a
corrected metric, and a fixture that was not used to define the metric.
Adopting it on the strength of the same data that revealed the mis-specification
is the failure the protocol exists to block.
- Earned: R04 — `verify=final` (tax 10 < 14; hole 1 → 0; guarded 5 > 4; quality
  identical) held back to R05 with a `hole_deliveries`-based clause:
  `ADR-ROUTER-V2-R04.md` ADR-16, `FINDINGS-R04.md` §The ruling.
- Enforced: the deferred variant stays an exercised, non-default mode; its
  corrected clause is written into the findings before the next round measures.
- Repeal if: a candidate passes its own pre-registered clause on a fresh
  fixture — then it ships and the superseded decision is dated in the ADR.
