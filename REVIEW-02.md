# REVIEW-02 — captain's value-testing review (2026-09-11, recorded verbatim in substance)

Thesis: ambitious, relatively coherent org design — but insufficient evidence the complexity buys results worth its cost. Not a demolition order: next phase must TEST value, not add detail. "Verified" in our files is operator-verified, not captain-verified. Replies follow PROJECT.md numbering.

## §0 — Measure existence less, effectiveness more
81 profiles ≠ 81 distinct behaviors ≠ 81 economically justified specialties. Dossier proves the first, partly the second, barely the third. 586 green tests cover the patch scope, not room effectiveness.
ORDER: project dashboard leads with real-mission results (quality/cost/time/human-intervention), not roster size.

## §1 — Ownership yes; no-sharing absolutism no
"One front per skull, never shares" is too harsh if it bans shared knowledge/review. Builder and reviewer must overlap in understanding; what differs is responsibility, incentives, check method.
ORDER: owner-per-responsibility; overlap allowed for review and continuity; no role repetition without proven added value. Weakness-as-price needs a necessity test: is the weakness really required by the strength, or a repairable flaw in a good story?

## §2 — Names don't prove independent minds
Same model in different characters guarantees style variance more than reasoning variance (gate/auditor/inspector/notary; historian/witness/recorder; translator/interpreter may be routing overhead).
ORDER: keep 81 as a specialty LIBRARY, not 81 jobs. Every role needs: input/output contract, authorities + prohibitions, call/skip conditions, and a delete-or-merge test on representative tasks. Start smallest, grow on measured need.

## §3 — Split constitution from harness
SOUL-stated permission/custody/handoff/stop rules are behavioral instructions, not enforcement guarantees. Critical rules (irreversible acts, read/exfiltrate permissions, dedupe, time-cost-attempt budgets, HOLD→execute transitions) belong in code, permissions, and a state machine.
ORDER: characters = thinking method, bias, critique style. Harness = permissions, routing, state, budget, logging, stop.

## §4 — Re-breaks may indict the design, not just the rules
"Every fix re-broken deeper" fits two theories: strong tests, or a complex org generating exceptions faster than closures. Summary can't pick.
ORDER: Trial-20 rescoped — per fix: claim → enforcement locus → behavioral test → result → guarantee limits. Conversational-only fixes labeled as such, never sold as enforcement. Arena 17:9 at ~6x cost is inconclusive: needs blind eval, replication, and user-value of the delta.

## §5 — Phase orders (all accepted)
(a) births frozen (need-proof over supply), (b) gateway stays gated (bounded tasks, hard budget, clear stop, human approval for sensitive external acts), (c) coach advisory-only (proposes, never edits; its changes tested on tasks separate from the lesson source), (d) rooms: 8 is capacity, start executor+reviewer and add planner on need, (e) self-redesign deferred (no performance record yet), (f) 10-books-per-entity deferred (start from a specific failure → targeted research → test), (g) Trial-20 scoped with a deadline, not an open audit door.

## Decisive experiment (captain's design)
12–20 real tasks from the intended usage; three formations (single well-equipped agent / 2–3-role small team / system-chosen formation); same tools and info, comparable budgets, real costs logged; blind evaluation; repeated tasks for variance. Metrics: user-acceptable quality, grave errors, time, cost, captain-intervention volume. New governing question: "did invoking it improve the outcome enough to justify its cost?"
TASK SOURCE (captain's decision, 2026-09-11): diverse distant benchmarks where model/agent performance was negative or very weak. Pack = locally-adapted proxies of known-weak classes (labeled as such, not official harnesses).
